## 1. World 09 — Lenia (Continuous Artificial Life)

### Mathematical Formulation
Developed by Bert Wang-Chak Chan, Lenia generalizes Conway's Game of Life into continuous space, continuous time, and continuous states $A(x, y) \in [0, 1]$.

1. **Spatial Convolution Potential**:
   $$U(x, y) = (K * A)(x, y) = \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2} K(dx, dy) \cdot A(x + dx, y + dy) \, dx \, dy$$
   Where $K(r)$ is a concentric ring kernel with radius $R$, centered at normalized radius $\mu_k$ with standard deviation $\sigma_k$:
   $$K(r) = \frac{1}{Z} \exp\left( -\frac{(r/R - \mu_k)^2}{2\sigma_k^2} \right), \quad r = \sqrt{dx^2 + dy^2} \le R$$
   Normalized such that $\iint K \, dx \, dy = 1$.

2. **Unimodal Growth Mapping**:
   $$G(u) = 2 \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{(u - \mu_g)^2}{2\sigma_g^2} \right) - 1, \quad G(u) \in [-1, 1]$$
   - $G(u) > 0$: Local growth / cell emergence.
   - $G(u) < 0$: Local decay / apoptosis.

3. **Continuous Time Integration**:
   $$A_{t + \Delta t}(x, y) = \text{clamp}\left( A_t(x, y) + \Delta t \cdot G(U(x, y)), 0, 1 \right)$$

### Key Parameters & Sliders
- **Growth Center ($\mu_g$)** $[0.08, 0.30]$: Optimum neighborhood potential supporting stable cell metabolism.
- **Growth Width ($\sigma_g$)** $[0.008, 0.045]$: Tolerance band of living tissue viability.
- **Kernel Radius ($R$)** $[8, 18]$ px: Neighborhood interaction horizon.
- **Environmental Coupling**: Dissolved Oxygen ($O_2$) accelerates integration rate $\Delta t$.

### Curated Presets
- **Orbium Soliton Glider**: Archetypal solitary self-organizing glider organism maintaining internal rotation and steady translational motion.
- **Continuous Slime Colony**: Interconnected biomorphic mass undergoing periodic morphological pulsation.

## 2. World 10 — Ferrofluid Dynamics (Rosensweig Instability)

### Mathematical Formulation
Ferrofluids are colloidal suspensions of ferromagnetic nanoparticles in a liquid carrier. When subjected to a vertical magnetic field exceeding the critical Rosensweig threshold $B_c$, the flat interface develops a hexagonal lattice of sharp conical peaks.

1. **Magnetic Potential Field**:
   $$\Phi(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{i=1}^{M} \frac{m_i}{\left( \|\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{d}_i\|^2 + \epsilon^2 \right)^{3/2}}$$
   Generated by orbiting internal dipoles $\mathbf{d}_i$ plus the interactive mouse cursor dipole.

2. **Interface Height Dynamics (Rosensweig Wave Equation)**:
   $$\frac{\partial^2 h}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 h - g h - \mu \frac{\partial h}{\partial t} + \alpha \max(0, |\nabla \Phi| - B_c)^2$$
   - Surface tension restoring force: $\gamma \nabla^2 h$.
   - Gravitational restoring force: $-g h$.
   - Viscous damping: $-\mu v_h$.
   - Superparamagnetic traction: Pulls fluid vertically into peaks where magnetic gradient is maximum.

3. **Specular Chrome Surface Rendering**:
   $$\mathbf{N} = \frac{(-\partial h/\partial x, -\partial h/\partial y, 1)}{\sqrt{(\partial h/\partial x)^2 + (\partial h/\partial y)^2 + 1}}$$
   Blinn-Phong illumination with halfway vector $\mathbf{H} = \text{normalize}(\mathbf{L} + \mathbf{V})$:
   $$I_{\text{spec}} = (\mathbf{N} \cdot \mathbf{H})^{26}$$
   Produces ultra-reflective metallic chrome highlights with iridescent chromatic oil-slick fringes.

## 3. World 11 — Fractal Dendrite (Diffusion-Limited Aggregation DLA)

### Mathematical Formulation
Witten & Sander (1981) model of non-equilibrium fractal cluster growth through irreversible aggregation of diffusive Brownian walkers.

1. **Brownian Motion**:
   $$\mathbf{r}_{t+1} = \mathbf{r}_t + \sqrt{2 D \Delta t} \cdot \boldsymbol{\eta}_t + \mathbf{g}_{\text{env}}$$
   Where $\boldsymbol{\eta}_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \mathbf{I})$ is Gaussian noise, $D$ is the diffusion coefficient scaled by ambient temperature.

2. **Irreversible Aggregation Criterion**:
   At each step, if walker $\mathbf{r}_i$ contacts any existing cluster cell within its 8-Moore neighborhood:
   $$P(\text{aggregate}) = P_{\text{stick}} \in [0.1, 1.0]$$
   Upon aggregation, the lattice cell records deposition timestamp $t_{\text{birth}}$. A new walker is immediately respawned on the perimeter bounding circle.

3. **Age-Gradient Chromatic Luminescence**:
   $$T_{\text{norm}} = \frac{t_{\text{birth}}}{t_{\text{current}}}$$
   Ancient inner trunk cores glow in deep ember gold, while young exterior growing branch tips radiate intense electric cyan.

## 4. World 12 — Excitable Medium Waves (Barkley Model)

### Mathematical Formulation
Coupled non-linear reaction-diffusion equations modeling excitable biological tissue (cardiac muscle electrical wave propagation, BZ chemical reaction):

1. **Kinetics**:
   $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{\epsilon} u(1 - u)\left(u - \frac{v + b}{a}\right) + D_u \nabla^2 u$$
   $$\frac{\partial v}{\partial t} = u - v$$
   - $u(x, y)$: Fast activator variable (transmembrane voltage / chemical excitation).
   - $v(x, y)$: Slow inhibitor variable (refractory state).
   - $\epsilon \ll 1$: Time-scale separation ratio ($\sim 0.02$).
   - $a, b$: Excitability and threshold parameters.

2. **Wave Collision Annihilation**:
   When two excitation wavefronts collide head-on, both annihilate because each wave enters the other's refractory tail ($v \gg 0$), preventing wave reflection or linear superposition.

3. **Scar Obstacle Refraction**:
   Right-click draws non-conductive barrier walls ($u = 0, v = 0$, Dirichlet condition), causing spiral rotor cores to pin and curl around obstacles.

## 5. World 13 — Rayleigh-Bénard Thermal Convection

### Mathematical Formulation
Boussinesq thermal-fluid equations coupling temperature transport with incompressible Navier-Stokes momentum equations:

1. **Incompressibility Constraint & Momentum**:
   $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} = 0$$
   $$\frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u} = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \nu \nabla^2 \mathbf{u} - \beta (T - T_0)\mathbf{g}$$

2. **Thermal Advection-Diffusion**:
   $$\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)T = \kappa \nabla^2 T$$

3. **Numerical Implementation**:
   - **Buoyancy Injection**: $v_y \leftarrow v_y - \beta (T - 0.5) \Delta t$.
   - **Chorin's Projection**: Poisson pressure equation $\nabla^2 p = \frac{\rho}{\Delta t}\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}^*$ solved via 8-pass iterative Jacobi relaxation to guarantee divergence-free flow.
   - **Semi-Lagrangian Advection**: Unconditionally stable backtracing along characteristic curves.
   - **Lagrangian Streamline Tracers**: 800 continuous particles advected by bilinear velocity interpolation.
